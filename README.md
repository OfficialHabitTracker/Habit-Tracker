# Habit Tracker 🎯

**Build better habits. Stay consistent. Track your progress.**

Habit Tracker is a web application designed to help you build daily routines, manage your habits, and monitor your progress over time.

🌐 **[Visit Habit Tracker]
https://habit-tracker-seven-ebon.vercel.app/

## ✨ What You Can Do

- **Track Daily Habits:** Keep your daily routines organized in one place.
- **Plan Ahead:** Manage habits planned for tomorrow.
- **Review Your Week:** Monitor your consistency and weekly progress.
- **View Your History:** Look back at your habit-tracking activity.
- **Manage Your Account:** Register and access your personal account.

## 💡 Why Habit Tracker?

Building habits is easy to plan but difficult to maintain. Habit Tracker gives you a dedicated place to organize your routines, record your progress, and stay accountable to your goals.

The goal is simple: make consistency easier to practise every day.
